# VoltPay marketplace customization notes

VoltPay is a static demo template. Replace the demo product claims, financial figures, store buttons, pricing CTAs, forms, branding, and legal destinations before using it for a real financial product.

## Before launch

- Replace demo exchange rates and connect the calculator to a real, trusted data source if required.
- Replace demo balances, transaction names, crypto prices, performance figures, security claims, and customer statistics with verified product information.
- Replace the App Store and Google Play buttons with real store URLs, or remove them if the product has no mobile app.
- Connect the early-access form to the buyer's chosen form/CRM/backend service.
- Replace pricing CTA buttons with the buyer's signup, checkout, or product onboarding destination.
- Replace footer Company and Legal destinations with real pages or remove links that do not apply.
- Replace the VoltPay favicon and social metadata if the template is rebranded.
- Replace the canonical URL and `robots.txt` sitemap URL when the template is deployed at another URL.

## Demo behavior

The exchange calculator uses simulated rates and a demo fee. The signup form is intentionally frontend-only and does not send data anywhere until connected by the buyer.
